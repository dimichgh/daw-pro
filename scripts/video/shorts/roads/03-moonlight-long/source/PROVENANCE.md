# Where these notes come from

`moonlight1-a4.ly` is the **Mutopia Project**'s LilyPond edition of

> Ludwig van Beethoven, Piano Sonata No. 14 in C-sharp minor, Op. 27 No. 2
> ("Moonlight"), 1st movement (Adagio sostenuto), 1801.

- **The composition is PUBLIC DOMAIN.** Beethoven died in 1827.
- **The typeset EDITION** is Mutopia-2007/02/11-276, engraved by Stewart Holmes
  from Berners, 1908 (ed. A. Winterberger), released under
  **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.5**.
- We synthesise the audio ourselves from these notes through our own
  instruments, so there is **no sound-recording copyright** involved.

⚠ **THE SHARE-ALIKE IS A DECISION FOR THE USER, NOT FOR ME.** Attribution is
carried here and in NOTES.md. If the finished film must avoid a share-alike
obligation entirely, the alternative is to re-derive the same public-domain
pitches from a non-CC source (or from a public-domain 1908-or-earlier scan) —
the pitches themselves cannot be copyrighted, only this particular engraving.
